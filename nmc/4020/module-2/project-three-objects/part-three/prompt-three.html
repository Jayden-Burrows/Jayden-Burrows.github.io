Case Study: Conflicting Theories on Aerith's Fate

Analyzing Narrative Mechanics, Multiversal Splinters, and Fan Theories in Final Fantasy VII Revelation

Jayden Burrows • NMC 4020 - Advanced Web Development

1. Project Overview & Background

Released in 1997, Final Fantasy VII revolutionized the video game industry by bringing Japanese Role-Playing Games (JRPGs) into the global mainstream through cinematic 3D graphics, intricate thematic storytelling, and the shocking, iconic tragic loss of its key heroine, Aerith Gainsborough.

Decades later, Square Enix initiated an ambitious reimagining with the Remake Trilogy—comprising Final Fantasy VII Remake (2020), Final Fantasy VII Rebirth (2024), and the upcoming Final Fantasy VII Revelation (2027). While maintaining the core narrative framework of the original game, this trilogy expands world-building and introduces crucial plot variations, altering player expectations surrounding fate and destiny.

2. Core Question

Will the main cast successfully change Aerith's fate, or is Cloud hallucinating her survival?

The central enigma framing the narrative climax of Final Fantasy VII Revelation centers on Aerith's ultimate state of existence: Will Cloud Strife and the surviving companions successfully rewrite fate to keep Aerith alive in physical reality, or is Cloud suffering from severe Jenova-induced hallucinations and psychological denial over her true death?

6. Theory Debate: Fate vs. Convergence

Theory A: Canonical Convergence (Aerith Is Dead)

Proponents argue that Aerith is physically deceased in the primary timeline. The scenes where Cloud sees her alive stem from his fractured psyche, severe PTSD, and Jenova cell manipulation meant to prevent him from grieving and keeping his mind unstable. In this view, Aerith exists solely within the Lifestream, comforting Cloud until he faces reality.

Theory B: Defying Destiny (Aerith Lives in Parallel)

Proponents suggest that when Cloud deflected Sephiroth's sword, he successfully split fate and created a new parallel world branch where Aerith survived, similar to Zack's timelines. Cloud's dual consciousness allows him to see across the Lifestream tear into this alternate reality. In Revelation, the party will merge or traverse these worlds to reunite fully.

7. Personal Assessment & Conclusion

My Stance: Aerith may be officially dead within the physical realm of the main cast's world—meaning she will never be able to physically reunite or journey with Cloud, Tifa, and the rest of the main group again. However, through the mechanics of the Lifestream and branching timelines, she will retain the opportunity to live out her life peacefully in a distinct parallel world.
