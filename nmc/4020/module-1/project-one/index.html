Common web-based attacks used by hackers and how developers can prevent them

Photo by Philipp Katzenberger on Unsplash

Introduction

Over time, websites have become a crucial part of life, enabling us to store personal information, process payments, communicate with others, and much more. As a result, it's also become more important for developers to maintain the security of their websites to prevent malicious third parties from exploiting vulnerabilities in their website in order to harm their users. Throughout this project, I'll discuss some attacks commonly used by hackers and the web programming practices developers employ to prevent them.
