I really enjoy reading too. I don't read as much as I used to, but I still read pretty often. I always recommend Gideon the Ninth whenever someone asks me for a book recommendation because it's a pretty fun book while still being mature. I would definitely say that the book and the rest of the trilogy are challenging to get through to say the least. Despite that, I would still highly recommend trying it out.

I also love reading comics and light novels because they can be pretty self-indulgent, and there's less pressure while reading them.
